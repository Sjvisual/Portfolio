Professional Profile

Engineering Background & Expertise

Sudhanshu Prakash Shashi is a backend-focused software engineer with over six years of hands-on experience across high-growth product companies, including Restroworks (formerly Posist), One97 Communications (Paytm), Caastle Technology, and Jugnoo / Click Labs Inc.

His work spans the full product lifecycle — from architecting scalable backend systems and RESTful APIs to leading engineering teams, conducting code reviews, and delivering production-grade features across distributed infrastructure.

Sudhanshu holds an M.Tech in Computer Science from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay and a B.E. in Computer Science from Kurukshetra University. In addition to his core engineering practice, he works as an independent AI Evaluator, assessing frontier language model outputs for reasoning quality, factual alignment, code correctness, and safety.

His technical foundation includes Python, Node.js, TypeScript, REST API design, Kafka, RabbitMQ, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch, Docker, AWS, and GCP. He has contributed to research projects at IIT Bombay in cross-language information access and storage management systems.
